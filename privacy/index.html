Pour the Sand Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what data is processed in the mobile game Pour the Sand (the "game"), published by Yusuf Ömer Erem ("we"). Questions: see the support page.

What the game itself stores

The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or phone number. Your progress (levels, coins, lives, settings, daily gift) is stored only on your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads, served by AppLovin MAX and the ad networks it mediates. To serve and measure ads, cap their frequency and prevent fraud, these companies may collect: device identifiers (on iOS the advertising identifier/IDFA only if you allow it, and the identifier for vendors/IDFV), IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country/city), device information (model, operating system, language, time zone) and your interactions with ads (impressions, clicks).

  • iOS tracking permission (ATT): on iPhone the advertising identifier is used only if you allow it. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: we ask for your consent to personalized ads. You can change your choice from Settings > Privacy in the game.
  • If you do not consent you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

AppLovin privacy policy: applovin.com/privacy

In-app purchases

Payments are handled by Apple (App Store); we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to validate and restore purchases. RevenueCat processes a random user ID that is not linked to your identity, your purchase history and basic device information. RevenueCat privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe this has happened, contact us and we will act on it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data and to object to its processing. We keep no data about you on our own servers; for data processed by our advertising and purchase partners, contact us through the support page or use the options in that company's policy.

Changes

We may update this policy; the current version is always on this page.
